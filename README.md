# Zeitspur — BerndSoft / Unteralterbach Research

Independent historical OSINT research and digital preservation concerning BerndSoft, Unteralterbach, and the surrounding German web communities.

## About

This repository documents an ongoing attempt to reconstruct the development history, contributors, aliases, online communities, and surviving digital artifacts associated with BerndSoft and the Unteralterbach project.

The research primarily uses contemporary and archived sources, including:

* Historical websites and web archives
* Recovered project and game files
* Contemporary forum and community posts
* Public correspondence
* Event records and recordings
* Historical software and digital artifacts
* Other publicly accessible primary or contemporary sources

## Research Principles

The project distinguishes between established evidence, supported findings, unresolved leads, and unverified claims.

A username match, repeated community claim, or similar online identifier is not treated as proof of identity by itself.

Primary artifacts and contemporary records are preferred over later recollections or repeated secondary claims.
